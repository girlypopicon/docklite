## Logo

The logo is a heart and a star in a cardboard box: `webapp/public/docklite-logo.svg` (also a 512px `docklite-logo.png` for the home-screen
icon and the two-factor QR code). The first logo, a heart balloon, is kept as `docs/brand/logo-heart-balloon-original.png`.

Theme background art (Vapor, Matrices, Girlypop) is drawn by `scripts/make-theme-art.py` into `webapp/public/themes/`.
